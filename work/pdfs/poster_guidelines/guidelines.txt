Examination: Research Poster

Deadline: 30.09.2026 (end of the day, central European time)
Group size: 1-2 participants (parallel group internal)

Summary: The poster must be based on empirical work related to a topic covered in the lecture,
seminar, or exercise component of the NLP module. It may extend your seminar presentation, but this
is not mandatory. There is no oral presentation. You must submit your poster digitally via STUD.IP,
together with a mandatory appendix. The following guidelines define all requirements. Read them
carefully.




Poster
The poster is the core submission. It must present empirical work in the field of NLP, structured into
the following three mandatory sections.

Hypothesis: State your research questions clearly. Situate your work within the broader research
context, explaining what prior work exists and where your contribution fits.
Methodology: Describe and justify the methods you employed, including the datasets, models, and
algorithms selected for your task. If your research involves technical implementations, you must
provide a link to an external repository (e.g. GitLab or GitHub) that allows us to reproduce your
experiments.
Results: Present your results using tabular and/or graphical representations. Discuss your findings
with respect to your original hypothesis, including any limitations or unexpected outcomes.

In addition, your poster must meet the following technical requirements:

   Format: DIN A1 (594 × 841 mm), portrait or landscape orientation
   Text: Readable in terms of color (sufficient contrast to background objects) and large enough font
   size for a poster (paragraph should be at least 24 pt)
   Figures: Readable in terms of resolution (pixel images should have at least 150 PPI)

    ⚠️ While the scientific content is our main grading criteria, we will also assess design and layout
    aspects including readability, spacing and visual concept (does the design help to strengthen
    your argument/content, or is it meaningless decoration?).




Appendix
The appendix is a separate, mandatory PDF document accompanying your poster. It must include the
following two components.

References: Provide a complete list of all sources consulted. References must primarily be academic
papers. A collection consisting solely of website links does not qualify as a valid reference list. Non-
academic sources are valid references in specific cases, including but not limited to: dataset
documentation or data papers, language model technical reports or model cards, news articles
relevant to your topic, official software or tool documentation.

Declaration of Academic Integrity: You must include a signed copy of the official university
declaration of academic integrity. The form is available for download from the University of Trier
website. Read the declaration carefully, as it regulates the permissible use of AI tools for examination
purposes. Download declaration (PDF)




Submission
Upload a single ZIP archive to the posters folder on STUD.IP within your seminar group. The
archive must contain exactly two PDF files: the poster and the appendix. The ZIP file must be named
according to the following convention, using your student ID(s). No deviations are accepted:
{student_id}.zip        or    {student_id_1}_{student_id_2}.zip (Example:          255678.zip     or
255678_123445.zip    ). The  submission  folder automatically closes after the deadline. We   do not
accept late submissions.
